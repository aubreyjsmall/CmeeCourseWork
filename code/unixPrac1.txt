#Count a literal motif. Count every occurrence of ATGC in the E. coli sequence, not just the number of lines containing it. Remove the header and line breaks first so wrapping cannot hide a match. grep -c counts matching lines; a match-only output such as grep -o lets you count occurrences. Keep ambiguous symbols in the sequence: deleting one could join separated bases and invent a match. The supplied motif cannot overlap with itself.
